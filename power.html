eXcontrol · how it works

Power, explained

What turns on what, and what turns off what. This works the same way in every eXcontrol installation, from one screen to a whole venue.

The principle

eXcontrol reaches your equipment in two ways

Power: it switches the mains supply on and off through an Expromo power unit (EPS). Network: it tells screens and processors to show a picture, go dark, sleep or wake up.

What turns on what

What Turn on, Standby and Turn off do

The same three buttons work for the whole room, or for part of it. What happens depends on how each piece of equipment is connected:

In what order

Switching on and off, step by step

Turn on up to a few minutes

1Power units on

One power unit at a time, each switch a moment after the last.

→
2Equipment starts

Processors and screens boot. eXcontrol waits until each one answers.

→
3Pictures on

Screens are woken one after another, and dark processors show the picture again.

Why one at a time? Equipment draws a large current spike at the moment it's switched on. Spreading the switch-ons out protects fuses and breakers.

Turn off a few seconds

1Screens sleep

Screens that keep their power (on a wall socket, or shared) are put to sleep over the network.

→
2Power units off

Last one first. Always-on equipment is left on.

Switching back on right after switching off waits at least 30 seconds, so the equipment isn't hard-restarted.

Parts of the room

Groups: control part of the room on its own

An installation can be split into groups, e.g. the main wall and the lobby screens. Each group has its own buttons and can have its own schedule. The room button always controls everything.

Who can switch it

Everything goes through eXcontrol

The power bar

Turn on, Standby and Turn off for the whole room. Turning off or to standby asks for a second tap, so it can't happen by accident.

The schedule

Switches the room, or a group, at set times. Before a scheduled switch-off you get a 15-minute warning and can extend by an hour.

Other control systems

Crestron, Q-SYS, Stream Deck and similar send their requests to eXcontrol, so the same safe order applies.

By hand, at the equipment

If someone turns a screen back on with a remote or a button, eXcontrol doesn't switch it off again. It shows a message saying what came on and when.
